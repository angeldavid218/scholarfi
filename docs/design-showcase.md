# ScholarFi Design Showcase

Use this checklist during investor rehearsal to compare the updated experience against the prior baseline.

## Screens to capture

1. `LoginPage` (`/login`)
2. `AdminHome` (`/admin`)

## Before/After capture protocol

1. Open each route in normal mode and take one screenshot.
2. Toggle **Modo presentacion** in the top bar and capture again.
3. Keep viewport at desktop width (`>= 1440px`) for investor deck consistency.

## What improved

- **Trust:** stronger governance framing and role-specific executive headers.
- **Impact:** KPI strips with operational context on every key dashboard.
- **Polish:** unified card hierarchy, spacing rhythm, empty states, and table treatment.
